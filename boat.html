ORIGAMI · Easy

Origami Boat

A symbol of peace and good luck in Japan. A great second model for kids and a calm fold for elders.

Level: EASY Time: 50 MINUTES Paper: 15 cm square

Steps

1. Gather all the patterned square papers, scissors, a glue stick, and a ruler.

2. Fold and crease the patterned papers into diamond shapes.

3. Assemble and arrange the folded pieces into a circular fan shape.

4. Prepare the individual folded components, head base, and body sections.

5. Combine the head, body, and fan tail to form the origami boat.

6. Your finished origami boat decoration is complete and ready to display.
