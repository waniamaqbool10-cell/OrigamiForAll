Paper Crane (Tsuru)

A symbol of peace and good luck in Japan. A great second model for kids and a calm fold for elders.

Level: EASY Time: 15 MINUTES Paper: 15 cm square

Steps

1. Start with a square sheet of traditional washi paper and make initial crease guidelines.

2. Fold the corners inward toward the center to form the initial kite shape.

3. Carefully press and flip the inner layers to align the structural folds.

4. Shape the main body structure by lifting and angling the wings upward.

5. Perform a small inside reverse fold on one of the tips to create the crane's head.

6. Gently open the wings to complete your traditional Japanese origami crane.
